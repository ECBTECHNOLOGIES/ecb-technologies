# Backlinks: 2–5 a month, reviewed after 3 months

The site ranks on low-competition searches without links, but a few good
links a month help the important pages (homepage, /laptop-shop-nairobi,
/research, /student-laptops-kenya). Build them by hand; never buy link
packages or join link networks.

What we can offer in return (our "linkable assets"):

- **Original price data**, updated automatically:
  [/research/laptop-prices-kenya](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya)
  and [/research/tech-prices-kenya](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/tech-prices-kenya).
  Journalists and bloggers can quote "median laptop price in Kenya" figures
  with a source link.
- **Buying advice backed by numbers**, for example "the 16 GB version of the
  same laptop costs a median of KSh 10,000 more" in
  [/learn/how-much-ram-laptop-kenya](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/learn/how-much-ram-laptop-kenya)
  and the [12-point used-laptop checklist](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/learn/check-used-laptop-before-buying).
- **Expert comment:** someone at the shop can answer journalists' questions
  about laptop prices, ex-UK laptops and what students should buy.
- **Offers for groups:** a student-union or staff discount code, or a talk on
  "how to check a used laptop".

## How to judge a link

- Good: a real Kenyan or tech site, with its own readers, that links in an
  article or a business listing relevant to laptops, phones or Nairobi.
- Avoid: "guest post" sites that publish anything for a fee, link farms,
  directories that list thousands of unrelated sites, and anyone selling
  "DA 50 backlinks".

## Track it

Keep a simple list (date, site, page linking, page linked to, status). After
3 months, compare Search Console clicks and impressions for the pages that
got links with those that didn't, and keep doing whatever worked.

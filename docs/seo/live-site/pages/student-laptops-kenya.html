What to look for in a student laptop

  • Processor: an Intel Core i5 or i7 of the 8th generation or newer. These are the oldest Intel processors Microsoft supports for Windows 11.
  • Memory: 8GB of RAM or more for browsing, Office and online classes at the same time.
  • Storage: an SSD, so the laptop starts and opens files quickly.
  • Size: 13 to 14 inches is easier to carry around campus; 15.6 inches gives a bigger screen.

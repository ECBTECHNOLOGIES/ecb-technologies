## Delivery across Kenya

We deliver laptops, phones, printers and accessories to all 47 counties in Kenya. In Nairobi, our own riders bring your order to you. Everywhere else, we send it by courier. You can also collect your order free from our shop.

## Three ways to get your order

**1. Rider delivery within Nairobi:** our riders deliver to your home or office anywhere in Nairobi County.

**2. Courier delivery to the rest of Kenya:** we send your order by courier to your town, in all 47 counties.

**3. Free pickup in Nairobi CBD:** collect from our shop at Techmart Iconic Business Plaza, 3rd Floor, Room T12, Moi Avenue, Nairobi CBD.

The delivery cost for your location is shown at checkout before you pay. For delivery times to your area, chat with us on WhatsApp: 0717 040 531.

## Rider delivery in Nairobi

Our riders cover all of Nairobi County, including Nairobi CBD, Westlands, Parklands, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Upper Hill, Karen, Lang'ata, South B, South C, Industrial Area, Eastleigh, Kasarani, Roysambu, Ruaraka, Embakasi, Donholm, Buruburu, Umoja, Kayole, Dagoretti, Kawangware, Kibra and Mathare.

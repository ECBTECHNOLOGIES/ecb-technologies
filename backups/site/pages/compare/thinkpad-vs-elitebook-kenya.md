## Which should you buy?

- **Tight budget:** ThinkPad. Its median is KSh 35,000 and the cheapest is KSh 16,000.
- **For multitasking and heavy apps:** ThinkPad (more of its models have 16 GB RAM or more).
- **For the longest software support:** EliteBook (more of its models have a 10th-gen or newer processor).

## Verdict

ThinkPad costs less at the median (KSh 35,000 against KSh 39,000). ThinkPad leads on multitasking and heavy apps; EliteBook leads on the longest software support; EliteBook leads on touch and pen use.

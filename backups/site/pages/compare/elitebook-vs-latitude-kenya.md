## Which should you buy?

- **Tight budget:** Latitude. Its median is KSh 28,000 and the cheapest is KSh 21,000.
- **For multitasking and heavy apps:** EliteBook (more of its models have 16 GB RAM or more).
- **For the longest software support:** EliteBook (more of its models have a 10th-gen or newer processor).
- **Highest spec available:** EliteBook, up to KSh 223,500.

## Verdict

Latitude costs less at the median (KSh 28,000 against KSh 39,000). EliteBook leads on multitasking and heavy apps; EliteBook leads on the longest software support. Either way, compare the exact model: the spread inside each group is wider than the gap between them.

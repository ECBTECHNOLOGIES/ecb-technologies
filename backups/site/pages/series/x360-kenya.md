## What the numbers say

74% of our HP x360 laptops have 16 GB of RAM or more, 74% have a 512 GB SSD or bigger, 91% have a touchscreen and 56% have a 10th-gen or newer processor (or an Apple M / Core Ultra chip). Moving from a 8th gen model (median KSh 50,000) to a 11th gen model (median KSh 57,000) is the biggest price step in this line. The cheapest is the HP PROBOOK 11E G2 CELERON 4GB 256 GB at KSh 21,000. 24% have a 7th-gen or older processor (or one we cannot confirm), which Windows 11 does not officially support; check the generation before you buy.

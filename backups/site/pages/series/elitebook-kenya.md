## What the numbers say

30% of our HP EliteBook laptops have 16 GB of RAM or more, 29% have a 512 GB SSD or bigger, 46% have a touchscreen and 56% have a 10th-gen or newer processor (or an Apple M / Core Ultra chip). Moving from a 4th gen model (median KSh 22,000) to a 11th gen model (median KSh 52,000) is the biggest price step in this line. The cheapest is the HP ELITEBOOK 820 G1 i5 4th Gen 8GB at KSh 22,000. 26% have a 7th-gen or older processor (or one we cannot confirm), which Windows 11 does not officially support; check the generation before you buy.

## How we ranked them

Each laptop gets a spec score out of 42, read from its listing: processor class ×3 (Celeron 1, Core i3 2, Core i5 3, Core i7 / Core Ultra 5 4, Core i9 / Core Ultra 7 / Apple M 5), processor age ×2 (Intel 7th gen and older 0–1, 8th–9th gen 3, 10th 4, 11th 5, 12th 6, 13th gen, Core Ultra and Apple M 7), memory (8 GB 3, 16 GB 6, 24 GB+ 8) and storage (256 GB 2, 512 GB 4, 1 TB+ 5).

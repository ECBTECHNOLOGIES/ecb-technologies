# Laptop Prices in Kenya 2026: 349 Laptops Analyzed

> The median laptop at ECB Technologies costs KSh 52,000, across 349 laptops priced from KSh 16,000 to KSh 324,000. 75 of 349 laptops (21%) cost under KSh 30,000; 62 (18%) cost KSh 120,000 or more.

Canonical: https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya
Last updated: 2026-11-25 (when the catalogue last changed)

## Key findings

1. The median laptop at ECB Technologies costs KSh 52,000, across 349 laptops priced from KSh 16,000 to KSh 324,000. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#median-laptop-price))
2. 75 of 349 laptops (21%) cost under KSh 30,000; 62 (18%) cost KSh 120,000 or more. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#laptops-under-30000))
3. The median Core i7 laptop costs KSh 52,000, KSh 21,000 more than the median Core i5 laptop (KSh 31,000); based on 132 Core i7 and 90 Core i5 laptops. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#core-i7-vs-core-i5))
4. HP makes 174 of the 349 laptops (50%), more than any other brand. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#most-common-laptop-brand))
5. Laptops with an Intel Core Ultra processor have a median price of KSh 145,500 across 46 models, the newest Intel generation in stock. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#core-ultra-laptop-price))
6. 47 laptops with an 8th-generation or newer Intel Core i5 or i7 cost KSh 40,000 or less; the cheapest is KSh 25,000. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#student-laptops-under-40000))
7. 145 of the 191 Core i5 and Core i7 laptops whose name states a generation (76%) are 8th generation or newer, the oldest Intel chips Windows 11 supports. ([link](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/research/laptop-prices-kenya#8th-gen-or-newer-share))

## Methodology

- Source: every product listed on www.ecbtechkenya.com, read from the shop's own catalogue at the time shown below.
- Prices are the current listed prices in Kenyan shillings, as shown on each product page.
- Excluded: listings without a usable price (under KSh 1,000 or over KSh 2,000,000) and duplicate listings of the same product.
- Brand, processor and generation come from the product names as typed by the shop; products whose name doesn't state them are left out of those tables.
- Findings with fewer than 20 products behind them are not shown; differences within 5% are reported as about the same.
- Medians are rounded to the nearest shilling; shares to whole percent (band and category shares add up to 100%).
- Refreshed automatically when the catalogue changes; data last changed 25 November 2026.

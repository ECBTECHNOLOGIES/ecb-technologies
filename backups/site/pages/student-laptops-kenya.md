## Where extra money goes furthest

1. **Processor generation first.** 8th gen is the minimum for Windows 11; 10th or 11th gen if your course is demanding. See [processor generations explained](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/learn/laptop-processor-generations-kenya).
2. **Then memory.** For the same model, 16 GB costs a median of KSh 10,000 more than 8 GB here. Worth it for computer science, engineering and design; not needed for most other courses. See [how much RAM you need](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/learn/how-much-ram-laptop-kenya).
3. **Storage last.** 256 GB is enough with cloud storage, and most business laptops can take a bigger SSD later. See [256 GB vs 512 GB](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/learn/laptop-ssd-size-kenya).

## Before you pay: a 5-minute check

- **Battery:** run `powercfg /batteryreport` and compare full-charge with design capacity (80%+ is good).
- **Windows 11:** Settings → System → About shows the processor; 8th gen or newer.
- **Keyboard, screen and ports:** type every key, look at a white screen for dead pixels, try every USB port.
- **BIOS:** no supervisor password at startup.
- **Warranty:** what it covers, for how long, written on the receipt.

The full list is in [how to check a used laptop before buying](https://www.ecbtechkenya.com/learn/check-used-laptop-before-buying). At our shop on Moi Avenue you can run these checks on any laptop before paying.
